## 📌 Softwares Developed

### [ViSPACE](https://github.com/himangi2003/ViSPACE)
End-to-end computational pathology pipeline for whole-slide image (WSI) analysis. It pairs the **Virchow2** foundation model with a pixel-wise segmentation decoder to produce five-class tissue maps (tumour, stroma, TILs, necrosis, other), then extracts spatial tumour microenvironment features: TSR and sTIL scoring, immune and necrosis proximity, and tumour morphology. Developed in 2026 under the mentorship of Dr. Joseph O. Deasy [ Deasy Lab ](https://deasylab.info) in Medical Physics at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. Version 0.1.0 is released, and the package is under active development with more features planned.

### [MultiDGEA](https://github.com/himangi2003/MultiDGEA)
R package for differential gene expression analysis of RNA-seq data across multiple time points. It runs five methods side by side (**limma-voom**, **limma-voom with duplicate correlation**, **DESeq2**, **kimma**, and **DREAM**) and compares their results with p-value and log-fold-change scatter plots and Venn diagrams of significant genes. Also includes heatmaps, volcano plots, and normalized-count plots. Developed in 2023 under the mentorship of [ Dr. Andrew Fiore-Gartland ](https://www.fredhutch.org/en/research/divisions/vaccine-infectious-disease-division/faculty-labs/vidd-staff-scientists-physicians/fiore-gartland-andrew.html) at the Vaccine and Infectious Disease Division (VIDD), Fred Hutch Cancer Center.

![R](https://img.shields.io/badge/R-276DC3?style=flat-square&logo=r&logoColor=white) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow?style=flat-square)


### [NetSeekR](https://github.com/himangi2003/NetSeekR)
R package for network analysis of RNA-seq time-series data. It covers alignment, differential expression, correlation and regulatory network inference, and network-based summaries for comparative genomics studies. Co-authored with Drew Ferrell. Developed in 2021 under the mentorship of Dr. George V. Popescu at the Institute for Genomics, Biocomputing & Biotechnology (IGBB), Mississippi State University ([lab GitHub](https://github.com/igbb-popescu-lab) · [original repository](https://github.com/igbb-popescu-lab/NetSeekR)).
